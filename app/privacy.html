People in the book

The book is a reference to the business of yacht racing: the people, teams, clubs, organisations and events in the sport, and how they connect. Many of the people in it have never signed up.

What we hold

Your name; your roles and relationships in the sport (for example "trimmer at a team", "chief executive of an event", "owner of a boat"), with dates where known; the nation you sail or work for; and the public source each relationship came from.

Where it comes from

Public sources about the sport: press releases, team, class and event websites, published results, and news reports. Readers and members also send corrections.

Why, and on what basis

We rely on legitimate interests: publishing an accurate reference to a professional sport and its business, about people in their public, professional roles. We don't include private life, home addresses or private contact details.

Your choices

Use Report an error on any entry to correct it. To see what we hold about you, or to object to being in the book, email privacy@darkbluebook.com. We'll weigh your request against the public interest in an accurate record of the sport, and tell you what we've decided and why.

If you apply to join

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and nationality (required).
  • Your roles in the sport (at least one).
  • If you give them: WhatsApp or mobile number, LinkedIn, Instagram and other social accounts, a short bio, your connection to the sport, who you've worked with and who referred you.

Why, and on what basis

To consider your application and, if you're accepted, to provide your membership: steps you ask us to take and the membership agreement between us. We use the links you give, LinkedIn especially, to check who you are.

Who sees it

  • The editors see everything on your application, and may write a private note about it, for example after contacting you.
  • Your sign-in email is never shown to anyone else.
  • If you're accepted, your name, nationality and roles appear in the book and on the map. Other profile details (photo, bio, social links, contact details) show only as your profile settings allow. Contact details are off unless you switch them on.

How long we keep it

  • If you don't confirm your email within a week, your application is deleted.
  • If you withdraw, or your application is declined, your application and account are deleted straight away.
  • If you're a member, we keep your details while you're a member. Ask us to close your account and we'll delete your profile and account. Your roles may stay in the book as editorial facts where we'd have included them anyway; you can object, as above.

Membership is for people aged 18 and over.

Reports and feedback

When you use Report an error or Send feedback we keep your message, your email address and, if you give it, your name, so the editors can act on it and reply. Only editors can read them. We delete reports once they're dealt with and no longer needed, and within 12 months of being closed.

Searches

When you use the search box we record which entry you open from it, and the words you typed when a search finds nothing, with the time. We don't record who searched: no account, IP address or device is stored with it. Editors use this to see who people look for and what's missing from the book. Only editors can see it, and we delete it after 12 months.

Signing in, and your browser

Sign-in uses a one-time link sent to your email; there are no passwords. Your browser stores your sign-in session and the view you last chose on the map. It also keeps a copy of the site and of the public book (the same entries, links, stories and public profiles anyone can see), plus photos and logos you've viewed, so the book opens quickly and works offline. Nothing from your account or other members' private details is kept this way. The copy is replaced each time you open the book online, so changes and removals reach your device on your next visit; clearing the site's data in your browser removes it. These are needed for the site to work, so we don't ask for cookie consent. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Like any website, our hosting providers log technical details of requests, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.
