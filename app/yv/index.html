How this directory works

Public profiles

Investors, customers and partners can browse every member startup: what they do, where they're based and who founded them.

Members see more

Signed-in members unlock stage, funding history and what each startup is looking for. Investors also see who's raising now.

Founders control their profile

Founders edit their own entry and choose whether to share it with The Dark Blue Book. Nothing is shared unless they opt in.

One record, many hats

People and companies already in The Dark Blue Book are linked, not copied, so their wider roles across the industry show here too.
